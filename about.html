About Apex Auto Club

Apex is a student project built around a car club concept. It brings vehicle comparisons, photography and a proposed event programme into one place.

What the club covers

Our focus is on how cars are designed and used: body styles, practical differences and the details owners notice over time. Meetings provide a setting to compare experiences and photograph cars.

Meeting guidelines

  • Ask the owner before touching or entering a vehicle.
  • Keep access roads and parking entrances clear.
  • Leave the meeting area clean.
  • Follow road rules and respect other visitors.
