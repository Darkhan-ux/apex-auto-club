Body-style comparison

A general comparison of coupes, sedans and hatchbacks. Space and equipment vary by model, generation and trim.

Layout and everyday use

Common body styles and example models
Body style Example Typical feature Practical consideration
Coupe Ford Mustang Two side doors and a low roofline Access to the rear seats
Sedan BMW 3 Series Four side doors and a separate boot Boot opening size for bulky items
Hatchback Volkswagen Golf A rear hatch that opens into the cabin Luggage space with the rear seats in use

Points to compare

  1. Passenger space and access to the rear seats.
  2. Boot size, opening shape and folding-seat layout.
  3. Overall dimensions and visibility when parking.
  4. Equipment and condition of the specific vehicle.
